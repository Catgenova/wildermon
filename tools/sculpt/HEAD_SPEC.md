Style: Stylized low-poly, flat-shaded 3D, with large clearly defined polygonal faces. No subdivision smoothing, realistic skin textures, or sculpted surface details.

Head structure
- Elongated masculine face with a broad upper skull and angular jaw.
- Bald skull with a naturally rounded silhouette constructed from large polygonal facets.
- Prominent geometric cheekbones, tapering into a narrow, flat-bottomed chin.
- Strong, straight brow ridge.
- Long, simplified neck with angular transitions.

Facial features
- Eyes: Two small, dark square pupils beneath slightly recessed angular eyelids. No detailed eyeballs.
- Eyebrows: Thick, straight, dark-brown geometric strips.
- Nose: Simple angular wedge with a broad planar bridge and small protruding tip. No visible nostril holes.
- Mouth: Minimal, nearly invisible, defined only through subtle geometry changes. No detailed lips.
- Ears: Angular, outward-projecting shapes with simplified inner surfaces.
- Expression: Neutral and relaxed.

Required modeling views — the exact same head from seven angles:
1. Front (0°) 2. Front three-quarter (45°) 3. Right profile (90°) 4. Rear three-quarter (135°) 5. Back (180°) 6. Top-down 7. Bottom-up.

Geometry and materials
- Target approximately 350–700 triangles for the head, excluding the neck.
- Use large planar polygons and deliberately faceted surfaces.
- Prioritize silhouette and recognizable planes over fine details.
- Use matte, medium-tan skin material with subtle warm color variations.
- Lighting should emphasize the polygon geometry with cool highlights on the left and warmer illumination on the right.
- No hair, facial hair, eyelashes, clothing, accessories, or decorative elements.

Reference sheet layout: front, three-quarter, profile, rear three-quarter and back in one horizontal row; top and bottom views underneath. Orthographic projection, matching head scale and neutral expression in every view. This is a 3D modeling reference, not separately designed portraits: every view must be the same exact mesh.
