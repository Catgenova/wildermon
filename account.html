Wildermon

A name and a password. That is the whole of it — there is no e-mail address to give, and nothing will ever be sent to you.

Three to twenty characters: a letter first, then letters, numbers, underscore or hyphen. It is what everybody on the island will see over your head, with the capitals you type. Signing in ignores capitals, so alice and Alice are one account, and signing in with different capitals changes them.

Eight characters at the very least, and checked against the list of passwords already known to have leaked. Neither it nor its full fingerprint leaves this page.

Two-step sign-in

Key:

Because there is no address to send anything to, there is no way to send you a reset link either. A forgotten password is a lost account. Write it down somewhere, or let the browser keep it.

The island wants a name it can put on a roster, so this is the way in. A name also keeps what you earn: every skill, every tool and the ground under your settlement hang off the account, and a Wanderer's hangs off a session this browser may tidy away.
